Stochastic
Parametric Simulations

Simulations of crowd behavior built from real-world data — maps, foot traffic, transit schedules, social media, weather, census, event listings, sales data, sensor networks — fused into deterministic models with dozens of constraints. Built for retail, transport, hospitality, education, urban planning, healthcare, logistics, entertainment, emergency response.

02

Models of Human Behaviour

We move well beyond the realm of simple chatbots.

Our work models emotional states, time- aware agent behaviour, “pretence mode” within fantasy settings, play dynamics, mental conditions, fuzzy memory processing and underlying mood structures.

These capabilities can translate into both text-driven and visually rich environments.

03

Robotic Models in Simulated Worlds

We fuse robotics, vision-language-action, and text models to power AI agents in rich simulated worlds—from global transport systems to games and education.

Our agents operate at any scale, with adjustable complexity—acting as individuals or coordinated groups.

04

Multidimensional Optimisation Problems

We solve optimisation problems—situations with many constraints where lots of solutions are possible, but only a few are actually good. Examples include planning logistics, choosing what to buy from many options, or designing flight routes. Most people deal with these kinds of problems every day even if they don’t know it.

These capabilities can translate into both text-driven and visually rich environments.
