About us

We are the Armenian Artificial Intelligence Company (AAIC)—a collective of scientists, engineers, designers, and writers. We work globally, creating unique software for diverse clients: from devices that assist professional chefs to city simulations and narrative games.

We are compiled of people who are self-deluding enough to believe—in this day and age—that software can be very fast, that new design languages can be developed and that design exists outside the field of graphics (as well as within).

Read more at Work Ethic and Ideology.

We are looking for colleagues. If you’re interested, but not on the list, go to Non-Jobs — we have those, too.

  • Job title: Opinionated Engineer with
    Experience in Rust
    Description:

    We're looking for an opinionated backend engineer whose language of choice is Rust.

    Most of our projects use TypeScript or Python. So why Rust? We believe it cultivates a sense of style and taste for clean, reliable code. We want engineers with strong opinions about what makes software good.

    We don't have product owners, scrum masters, daily stand-ups, or grooming sessions via Zoom. Instead, we have project briefs and debriefs, critical path-based planning, and PERT.

    We leverage AI at every level of our work—from task tracking to writing code. We expect modern engineers to do the same.

    This position is hybrid: one week each month, we gather in our Yerevan office for an intensive creative burst. The rest is remote. Fully remote is also possible.

    The team language is Russian; our projects are in English. Being eloquent in the former and proficient in the latter is a hard requirement.

    The pay is $3,000–4,000 USD after taxes (depending on tax residence).

    What's next?

    1. A call where someone will introduce our company and you'll have a chance to ask questions.
    2. An unusual test problem where we'll present you with a challenge in a fantasy project, have you ask questions, and ideate a solution.
    3. A second call where you'll present your solution, after which we'll make a final decision.

    Because of payment issues, we can't work with people physically located in China, Russia, Belarus, Iran, or other sanctioned countries.

    If you need to anonymize your application, go ahead.

    To apply, write an email to aarm@aicompany.am with the following:

    • Your CV (we care about the work you've done, not big names).
    • A link to your GitHub account.
    • A joke in each language.
    • Your opinion on the best IDE/model for AI-assisted engineering.
    • A brief description of a project where you made a significant contribution.

    Focus on what made it special for you. As an AI company, we're very good at spotting LLM writing. A human will read your application and always respond by hand, so please extend the same courtesy.
