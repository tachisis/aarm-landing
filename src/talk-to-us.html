Sales Call

An AAIC sales representative will join you to share relevant business and technical expertise, and to walk you through our usual sales process and portfolio.

They can outline typical cost brackets, expected timelines, standard contracts, and of course answer any specific questions you may have.

If you raise a particular opportunity that we might explore together, they will follow up by email with the relevant details.

Job Interview

Please do not use the buttons below to arrange a job interview.

We review every application submitted through the Jobs and Non-Jobs pages with care. If one is a fit and there is mutual interest, everyone receives a response.

There is no need to follow up or remind us. We are receiving a perfectly manageable number of applications and will be in touch in due course.

Wine Call

One of us will happily share a glass of wine or a beer with you. As before, there is no pitch and no follow-up unless you specifically ask for one.

We always find this format useful to brainstorm, offer informal advice at no cost, or simply put two heads together on your next initiative, collaboration, or idea.
